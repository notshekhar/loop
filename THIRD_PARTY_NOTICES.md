# Third-party notices

loop includes material from the following projects.

## cuelume

The embedded interface chimes (`packages/core/src/notifications/assets/`, and
the generated `sounds-blob.ts` built from them) are derived from the sound
recipes in [cuelume](https://github.com/Danilaa1/cuelume) by Daniel Belyi. The
rendered 48 kHz AAC files were taken from
[vercel-labs/fx](https://github.com/vercel-labs/fx), which produced them from
those recipes; `click.caf` is cuelume's original IMA4 CAF, which has no recipe
to re-render from.
